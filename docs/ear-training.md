# Ear training and reading quizzes

[← Manual](README.md)

Four exercises made of **blocks of questions** (10 by default, changeable in
[Settings > Exercises](settings.md#exercises)):

| Practice menu | Question | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| **Intervals (ear training)** | An interval is played | Its name |
| **Interval reading (staff)** | Two notes on the staff | The interval |
| **Chord qualities (ear training)** | A chord is played | Its quality |
| **Fingerings & note names** | One note on the staff | The valves, or the note name |

## How a block works

- **Start**: all the questions of the block are drawn, then asked one by one. **Start**
  becomes **Pause / Resume**.
- Answer with the buttons. After the answer the app says right or wrong (and, for ear
  training, plays the question again) and goes on by itself.
- **Stop** ends the block; the questions not answered count 0.
- **Repeat** = the **same questions in another order** (the same order would be too easy):
  one more attempt on the same exercise.
- **Keep** puts the exercise in Objects at once (it goes there anyway at the end).
- The clock shows the time of the current question and the total (pauses excluded).
- At the end the block becomes an exercise ◆ in Objects with its grade; double-click it to
  do the same questions again.
- **History** shows the results over time and, for intervals, the accuracy for each
  interval.

Points and grade: see [Grading > Blocks of questions](grading.md#blocks-of-questions).

## Intervals

| Option | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Intervals** | Which ones to ask (the minor second is off by default). |
| **Mode** | Harmonic (both notes together), melodic up, melodic down, mixed. Harmonic intervals are worth 1.2×. |
| **Instrument** | Sound of the question. |

A harmonic interval is played twice. After a **wrong** answer you hear it again, harmonic
then melodic, with its name. **Replay** hears it again during the question (it costs
points).

## Interval reading

| Option | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Answer** | *Number* only (2nd … octave) or *number + quality* (m2 … P8; A4 and d5 spelled correctly). |
| **Accidentals** | Notes with sharps and flats. |
| **Clef** | Treble or bass. |
| **Notes** | Melodic up, melodic down, harmonic. |
| **Play after answering** | Hear the interval once you have answered. |

## Chord qualities

| Option | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Qualities** | major, minor, diminished, augmented, sus4, dominant 7th, major 7th, minor 7th, half-diminished, diminished 7th, 6, m6, m(maj7). |
| **Mode** | Block chord or arpeggio. |
| **Inversions** | Not only in root position. |
| **Instrument** | Sound of the chord. |

After a **wrong** answer the chord is played again as a block and then as an arpeggio; the
next question waits until the arpeggio has finished, plus one second.

## Fingerings & note names

One note on the treble staff, for the B♭ trumpet (written pitch).

| Option | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Answer** | *Fingering*: the valves (0 2 1 12 23 13 123, the standard chart). *Note name*: one of the 12 notes (enharmonics are equal: C♯ = D♭). |
| **Accidentals** | Notes with sharps / flats (spelled at random). |
| **Lowest / Highest** | Range (written). |
| **Play after answering** | Hear the note. |
